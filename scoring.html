1The short version

Your badge is decided by three scored events: the Basic Fitness Test (BFT), pistol and ruck march. Each earns a level of Bronze, Silver or Gold.

Your badge is the lowest of those three levels. A Gold BFT, a Silver pistol score and a Bronze ruck earns Bronze.

You also have to meet every BFT minimum and pass all three pass/fail events (first aid, NBC/MOPP4 and the swim). Missing any of them means no badge.

2BFT minimums

The three BFT events are done in this order within 90 minutes. Missing any minimum fails the BFT, regardless of age or gender.

EventMinimum to pass
11 × 10 m sprint60.0 sec or faster
Flexed-arm hang5.0 sec or longer
1000 m run6:30 or faster

3Earning points

Each event starts at 100 points at the minimum. Better performance earns more points on a straight-line scale.

  • Sprint: 15 points for every second faster than 60. Your exact time counts, so 41.3 s earns 380.5 basic points.
  • Flexed-arm hang: 5 points for every second held past 5. Your exact time counts, so 52.7 s earns 338.5 basic points.
  • 1000 m run: 100 points for every 55 seconds faster than 6:30, or about 1.8 points per second. Times are in whole seconds.

4Age and gender bonuses

Bonuses are calculated from your basic points and added to each event.

Age (36 and older)

age bonus = basic points × (age − 35) × 0.005

That's 0.5% per year past 35. A 40-year-old gets +2.5% on each event.

Female

  • Sprint: +15% of basic points
  • Flexed-arm hang: +40% of basic points
  • 1000 m run: +15% of basic points
event score = basic + age bonus + gender bonus, rounded (max 500)

Worked example

A 40-year-old female holds the flexed-arm hang for 45.6 seconds, which earns 303 basic points.

  • Age bonus: 303 × 5 × 0.005 = 7.575
  • Gender bonus: 303 × 0.40 = 121.2
  • Event score: 303 + 7.575 + 121.2 = 431.775, which rounds to 432

5BFT average and levels

Your three event scores are averaged and rounded to the nearest whole number. For example, scores of 417, 400 and 382 average 399.67, which rounds to 400 for Gold.

6Pass / fail events

These don't affect your level, but failing any one means no badge.

  • First aid: Combat Life Saver (or equivalent) within the last 3 years, certified by your leadership.
  • NBC / MOPP4: put on the protective mask in 9 seconds and go to MOPP4, passed within the last year and certified by your leadership.
  • 100 m swim in uniform: swim 100 m in under 4 minutes, then undress in deep water without support. Any stroke is allowed, and there's no time limit for undressing.

7Pistol (P-ES-6)

Ten rounds fired standing from the low-ready position. Each hit inside the scoring zones and within the time limit is 1 point. Rounds fired after time expires count as misses.

8Ruck march

March with a 15 kg (33 lb) pack. The distance you complete sets your level, and you must finish within its time limit, which works out to 10 minutes per kilometer.

DistanceTime limitLevel
6 km (3.73 mi)1:00:00Bronze
9 km (5.6 mi)1:30:00Silver
12 km (7.46 mi)2:00:00Gold

Finishing over the time limit means no badge.
